# Week 2026-W42 (Oct 12–18) — Build Phase 1, Week 2

## Context

**Build 1 loading week 2 — run VO2 + sweet-spot week.** The VO2 stimulus this week is on the
**run** — your first *dedicated* VO2 session — while the bike does sustained **sweet-spot**.
VO2 is **offset week-to-week between run and bike** (W41 bike VO2, W42 run VO2, W43 bike VO2),
so you never stack two VO2 sessions, or a VO2 on top of a big tempo block, in one week — each
is run fresh and focused. _Why run VO2 is the smaller of the two doses: a 70.3 run is raced at
tempo/easy pace and your run ceiling is already high (sub-20 5k), so the biggest VO2 yield is
on the bike (it lifts FTP → race watts). Run VO2 here maintains the ceiling + economy; it's
real work, just not the main lever._ This is also a **brick week**: Saturday's long ride gets a
short transition run off the bike (bricks are now a regular Build fixture). **The Friday run
drops** in brick weeks to stay at max 3 runs/week.

**⚠️ FTP still 238W** (clean retest is W44). Prescribe to the bands below.

- Bike: Z2 = **132–179W** · Sweet-spot = **209–224W** (88–94%)
- Run: Z2 = **135–155 bpm** (~5:00–5:45/km) · Z3 tempo = **156–168 bpm** (~4:35–5:00/km)

### Tue Oct 14
**Morning — Run, VO2 (dedicated — your first real VO2 session)** (~55–60min)

Warm up 15min easy + **4 × 20s strides** to open the legs. Then **5 × 3min @ Z5 VO2** (179+ bpm,
~4:00–4:15/km — your ~3–5k effort, *controlled*, not a sprint), **3min easy jog between**
(near-full recovery — the rest is what lets you hit true VO2 on the next rep). Cool down 10min.
~15min of quality, run **fresh** — that's the whole point of giving VO2 its own day instead of
bolting it onto a tempo block. Hold the pace even across all five; if rep 5 falls off badly,
stop at 4 — quality over quantity. Progress to 6×3 or 5×4 in later blocks.

_Hill option (lower impact, builds strength — good if the legs are at all beat up post-camp):
**5 × 3min uphill** at hard-but-controlled effort, jog down to recover._

**Afternoon — Easy ride** (~60min) — Z2, genuinely easy (RPE 3–4). Leg flush + volume.
_Note: VO2 run Tue sits ~24h before Wed heavy legs — if the legs feel trashed Wed morning,
back the squat load off rather than grinding it._
