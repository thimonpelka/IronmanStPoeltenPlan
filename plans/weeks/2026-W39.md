# Week 2026-W39 (Sep 21–27) — Base Phase, Week 13 — BEACH VOLLEYBALL CAMP

## Context

**Full-week beach volleyball camp (Sep 21 → 28), ~2 sessions/day.** No planned triathlon
training. This is a high physical-load, high-injury-risk week in a completely different
modality — stacking swim/bike/run on top of 2-a-day beach volleyball is how ankles and knees
get hurt (macro *Load Management*: VB is real load **and** real injury risk).

**This week deliberately absorbs the deload role** for the W37→W38 block (macro: deloads flex
±1 week around real-life perturbations). Treat it as an unload from triathlon, *not* as rest —
the body is working hard, just differently.

---

## Plan

**No structured triathlon sessions.** Guidance for the week:

- **Optional easy run only** — one gentle 30–40min Z1 jog mid-week **if and only if** you feel
  fresh and there are no niggles. It's a "if you feel like it" session, not a target. Skip it
  without a second thought if the legs/joints are tired from VB.
- **No swim / bike / gym.** Let the camp be the load.
- **Protect the joints**: warm up properly for VB, listen to ankles/knees, treat any tweak as
  a red flag (macro rule) — a strain here derails months of triathlon build. Don't "train
  through" a niggle.
- **Recover actively**: hydrate (heat + volume), fuel enough protein and carbs, sleep. You're
  training ~6h/day of explosive sport.
- **Log the VB sessions to Strava** so the load is visible when planning the W40 re-entry.

---

## Return to training

Camp ends Mon Sep 28 (last day). Triathlon training resumes **W40 as a cautious re-entry**
(see `plans/weeks/2026-W40.md`) — easy aerobic + re-establish swim frequency first, intensity
only once the body (and any camp niggles) says it's ready. Do **not** launch Build 1 hard
straight off the camp.
