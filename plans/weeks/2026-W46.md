### Tue Nov 10
**Morning — Run, VO2 (progressed to 6 × 3min)** (~60min)

Warm up 15min easy + **4 × 20s strides**. Then **6 × 3min @ Z5 VO2** (179+ bpm, ~4:00–4:15/km —

quantity. _Hill option if legs are beat up: 6 × 3min uphill hard-but-controlled, jog down._

**Afternoon — Easy ride** (~60min) — Z2, genuinely easy (RPE 3–4). Flush + volume.
