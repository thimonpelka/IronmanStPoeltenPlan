# Week 2026-W38 (Sep 14–20) — Base Phase, Week 14

## Context

**Last full training week before the beach volleyball camp (Sep 21–28).** Small progression
on W37, then a **deliberately eased weekend** so you arrive at the camp fresh — 2 VB
sessions/day on trashed legs is the injury scenario the macro warns about, and VB injury risk
is the priority over squeezing one more long session.

Two priorities:
1. **Get the last open-water swim in on Friday** (weather permitting) — after the camp, the
   Vienna OW season is basically closing. This is likely OW session 3 of block 1.
2. **Peak sweet-spot session Thursday** (2 × 20min) — the top of this short base block before
   the camp interrupts.

**⚠️ Zones** — same caveat as W37: recompute from the Sep 5 FTP retest if it moved. Below
assumes FTP 238W.
- Bike: Z2 = **132–179W** · Sweet-spot = **209–224W**
- Run: Z2 = **135–155 bpm** (~5:00–5:45/km) · Z3 tempo = **156–168 bpm** (~4:35–5:00/km)

## Schedule

blocks than W37.

Cool down. Slightly longer sustained blocks than W37's 3×10.

**Afternoon — Easy ride** (~60–70min) — Z2, genuinely easy (RPE 3–4). Leg flush + volume.

### Wed Sep 16
**06:30 — Gym — Lower body, HEAVY** (~60min) — last heavy lower before the camp; squat 4 × 5,
RDL, split squat, calf, core. Progress load only if W37 reps were clean. See `plans/strength.md`.

**Evening — Swim, aerobic / CSS-pace** (~60min, ~2200m) — the quality swim; progression on
W37's 10×100 toward longer reps.

- Warm-up: 300m easy · Drills: 6 × 50m
- **Main set: 8 × 150m @ CSS-pace** (comfortably hard, repeatable), 20s rest
- 4 × 50m building to fast, 20s rest
- Cool-down: 200m easy

_Longer reps than W37 = a touch harder to hold pace. If form frays, take 30s rest or cut to
6×150 — quality over quantity._

### Thu Sep 17
**Midday/afternoon — Mid-week ride, sweet-spot (peak session)** (~110min) — *protect this*

Warm up 15min Z2. Then **2 × 20min @ sweet-spot (209–224W)**, 6min easy between. Cool down
15min. The longest sustained sweet-spot of the block. If the second 20 sags below 205W, that's
the honest ceiling — record it (feeds the FTP picture).

**Afternoon (after the ride) — Gym — Upper + core** (~50min) — push/pull/overhead + core.

**Easy run** (~30–35min) — Z1–low Z2, recovery pace. Skip if legs are flat.
