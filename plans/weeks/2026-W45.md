# Week 2026-W45 (Nov 2–8) — Build Phase 1, triple 2 week 1 (sweet-spot + threshold)

## Context

**First week of the second Build 1 loading triple (W45→W47), deload closes it in W48.**
Re-launch the progression after the deload: ease back into quality with a **sweet-spot bike** and
a **threshold run**, rebuild the long run, then W46/W47 carry the harder VO2 doses. Quality
pattern across the triple (locked template): **bike = 2 sweet-spot + 1 VO2**, **run = 2 threshold
+ 1 VO2**, VO2 offset so the two never share a week (run VO2 W46, bike VO2 W47).

**FTP stays 238W — no retest.** (Decided 2026-11-02: zones are tracking fine on what you've been
training on, and should be roughly where they were two months ago, so there's no point
re-testing now.) The bands below hold; sanity-check against RPE/HR on the trainer as always.

**🏐 New standing change (from this week, through ~end of March): Friday beach volleyball
12:00–14:00.** The **Friday easy run is dropped** for the whole stretch — volleyball is the
Friday leg load. Most Build/Peak weeks are brick weeks anyway (Friday run already dropped), so
this mostly just formalizes it. The Friday easy swim stays (before volleyball or after — your
call).

- Bike (FTP 238W): Z2 = **132–179W** · Sweet-spot = **209–224W** (88–94%) · VO2 = **250–270W**
- Run: Z2 = **135–155 bpm** (~5:00–5:45/km) · Z3 tempo = **156–168 bpm** (~4:35–5:00/km)
- Swim: **CSS = 1:43/100m** (from the W41 test, Oct 7) — prescribe CSS sets off this.
