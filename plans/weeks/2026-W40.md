# Week 2026-W40 (Sep 28–Oct 4) — Build Phase 1, Week 1 — CAUTIOUS RE-ENTRY

## Context

**First week of Build 1 — but treated as a re-entry, not a launch.** You're coming straight
off a week of 2-a-day beach volleyball (camp ends Mon Sep 28), so the legs may be fatigued
and there's real niggle risk. Slamming into Build 1's sweet-spot/VO2 progression now would be
a good way to get hurt or dig a hole. So W40:

- **Re-establishes frequency before intensity** — get the 3 swims / 3 rides / 3 runs rhythm

- **No VO2 yet.** The macro's every-other-week VO2 stimulus (Build 1) starts once you're
  clearly recovered — W41 at the earliest.
- **Checks for camp niggles** before any hard effort. Any ankle/knee/lower-back tweak → keep
  it all easy and flag it.
