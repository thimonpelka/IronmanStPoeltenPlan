# Week 2026-W35 (Aug 24–30) — Base Phase, Week 11

## Context

**Peak load week of the W33–35 triple** — the biggest week of the late-base block. Next
week (W36) is a deload with two field tests, so push here knowing recovery + a reset are
scheduled. Two progressions this week:

1. **Second brick** (Sat) — the long ride gets a longer transition run bolted on (15min in
   W31 → ~20–25min now). Running off the bike is the race-specific durability skill.
2. **Longest sweet-spot block yet** (Thu) — building the mid-week bike's sustained power.

Swim stays 3× (Tue pool / Wed pool / Fri open water).

**Zones** (FTP 238W): Bike Z2 = 132–179W · Sweet-spot = 209–224W.
Run Z2 = 135–155 bpm (HRmax 192 / LTHR ~178) · Z3 tempo = 156–168 bpm.

## Schedule

### Mon Aug 24
**Gym — Upper + core** (flexible time, ~55min)

Push/pull/overhead + core + stability. Low leg load. See `plans/strength.md`.

---

### Tue Aug 25
**Morning Run — Tempo** (~62min)

Warm up 15min. Then: **2 × 18min @ Z3 tempo** (155–169 bpm), 4min easy jog between. **4 ×
20s strides**. Cool down 8min. Longest sustained tempo of the block — this is fatigue-
resistance work, keep it controlled at the *top of Z2 / low Z3*, not a grind.

**Afternoon — Swim (pool)** (~50min) — warm-up 300m, 4 × 50m drill, **4 × 200m** or
**2 × 400m continuous** (rest 30–40s), 100m cool-down. ~1400–1500m. Longest continuous
swimming yet if form holds.

---

### Wed Aug 26
**06:30 — Gym — Lower body, HEAVY (week 3 / top of block)** (~60min)

Squat 4 × 5 (heaviest of the block if reps are clean), RDL 3 × 6, Bulgarian split squat
3 × 8/leg, calf 3 × 12, core. 1–2 reps in reserve — this is the last heavy lower session
before the W36 deload. See `plans/strength.md`.

**Evening — Swim (pool)** (~45min) — easy drills + rhythm, ~1100m. Keep it light on lifted
legs; today's priority was the squat session.

---

### Thu Aug 27
**Mid-Week Ride — Sweet-Spot** (~95–105min) — *protect this session*

Warm up 15min Z2. Then: **2 × 20min @ sweet-spot (209–224W)**, 6min easy between. Cool
down 15min. The longest continuous sweet-spot blocks of the block. Hold the band; if the
second 20min sags below ~205W, end it there and log the drop — it calibrates the FTP
retest expectation.

---

### Fri Aug 28
**Open Water Swim** (~40–45min) — OW block 1.

Sighting, straight-line segments, calm breathing. Try one longer continuous straight-line
stretch (~150–200m) between sighting drills. Someone present; get out if cold.

_Note: the macro pencilled an FTP retest for ~Aug 28 — it's been moved to the W36 deload
(Sep 1) so it lands on fresh legs. See below._

---

### Sat Aug 29
**Second Brick** — Long Ride + transition run — **rolling by 07:00–07:30** (heat rule; the
run lands ~10:00, before the worst heat).

- **Ride**: Z2 endurance (132–179W, ~150–170W), ~2:40h, ~80–90km, real elevation where
  possible — watts into Z3 on climbs, flats stay Z2. Fuel 60–80g carbs/h from 45min.
- **Immediately off the bike** (<5min transition): **~20–25min easy run**, Z2. Longer than
  the W31 first brick (15min). Legs will feel heavy early then settle — that's the
  adaptation. Keep it easy, don't chase pace.

---

### Sun Aug 30
**Long Run** — Z2, ~18–19km, ~1:40–1:45h — **start by 07:00–07:30** (heat rule).

Legs carry brick fatigue from Saturday. Easy Z2 (135–155 bpm), run by feel. Slightly
shorter than a standalone long run because of the brick load the day before.
